🍔 Burger King - Store Sales Dataset (2026)

Sales from one Burger King outlet, January to March 2026.

Goal: See how sales change month by month and which items and categories bring in the most revenue.

Files

menu.csv: 32 menu items with their category and price
order_details.csv: every item sold, with order ID, date and time

Connect them with order_details.item_id → menu.menu_item_id. Revenue comes from the menu price.
